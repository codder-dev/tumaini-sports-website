Annual Sports Day 2024 - A Huge Success!

June 15, 2024 By Admin 5 min read
The team in their annual sports

Over 200 children participated in our annual sports day event filled with football, athletics, and fun activities. The community came together to celebrate talent and teamwork.

On Saturday, June 15, 2024, Tumaini Sports Initiative hosted its annual Sports Day at Mbagathi Road Primary School. The event was a resounding success, bringing together children, parents, coaches, and community members for a day of celebration, competition, and community spirit.

A Day of Celebration

The day kicked off with a colorful opening ceremony featuring a parade of participating teams, each representing different communities from across Nairobi. The atmosphere was electric as children waved flags and cheered for their teams.

Exciting football matches
Thrilling athletics races

Football & Athletics Competitions

The main events of the day were the football and athletics competitions. Young athletes showcased their skills in football matches, relay races, sprint events, and field events like long jump and shot put. The level of talent on display was truly inspiring.

"Seeing the children's joy and determination on the field reminds us why we do what we do. Sports has the power to transform lives."

— Willis Otieno, Team Leader, TSI

Community Support

We were honored to have representatives from AMREF, ACAKORO Football Academy, and Athletics Kenya in attendance. Their support and encouragement meant a lot to the children and our organization.

Community members and partners showing support

Celebrating Achievements

At the end of the day, medals and trophies were awarded to the winners and participants. But the real victory was seeing every child leave with a smile, a sense of accomplishment, and memories to last a lifetime.

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to everyone who made this day possible - our coaches, volunteers, parents, partners, and most importantly, the children who continue to inspire us every day.
