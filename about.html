Who We Are

Introduction to Tumaini Sports Initiative

Tumaini Sports Initiative (T.S.I) is a registered Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) whose vision is to improve health, wellness and education for children in the informal settlements through sports.

T.S.I uses sports as a means of reaching the poor children through health and education services while building their confidence and a sense of self-worthiness to enable them to lead a better life and to bring about a lasting change in their lives and their vulnerable families and be productive citizens.
