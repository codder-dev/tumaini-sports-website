Coaching Workshop 2024 - Empowering Our Coaches

May 28, 2024 By Admin 4 min read
Students enjoying after seeing their hardwork paying off

Empowering our coaches with new skills and techniques for better training. Our annual coaching workshop brought together coaches from across Nairobi.

From May 26-28, 2024, Tumaini Sports Initiative hosted its annual Coaching Workshop at Mbagathi Road Primary School. The workshop brought together 25 coaches from across Nairobi to enhance their skills, share best practices, and learn new training methodologies.

Building Better Coaches

The workshop focused on equipping coaches with modern training techniques, child psychology, injury prevention, and effective communication skills. The sessions were led by experienced trainers from Athletics Kenya and ACAKORO Football Academy.

Coaches teaching his player
Photo of Coaches and the team

Key Workshop Highlights

  • Modern Training Techniques: Introduction to age-appropriate training methods
  • Child Psychology: Understanding and motivating young athletes
  • Injury Prevention: Safe training practices and first aid basics
  • Communication Skills: Effective coaching communication
  • Team Management: Building cohesive teams

"A great coach is not just someone who teaches skills, but someone who inspires, motivates, and builds character. This workshop has equipped our coaches to do exactly that."

— Frank Murithi, Head Coach, TSI

Practical Sessions

Coaches participated in practical sessions on the field, applying what they learned in real training scenarios. They worked with children from the program, receiving feedback and guidance from master trainers.

Coaches in action during practical sessions

Certification and Closing

All participating coaches received certificates of completion. The workshop ended with a commitment to implement the new techniques in their training programs and a plan for continued professional development.

We are confident that this workshop will have a lasting impact on the quality of coaching our children receive, ultimately leading to better development outcomes.
