New Partnership with AMREF Announced

We are thrilled to announce our new partnership to expand health and wellness programs for children

June 5, 2024 By Willis Otieno 4 min read
The partnership signing ceremony between TSI and AMREF

We are thrilled to announce our new partnership with AMREF to expand health and wellness programs for children in informal settlements.

On June 5, 2024, Tumaini Sports Initiative (TSI) and the African Medical Research Foundation (AMREF) formalized a partnership that will bring comprehensive health and wellness services to children in informal settlements across Nairobi.

A Milestone Partnership

This partnership marks a significant milestone in our mission to improve the health, wellness, and education of children through sports. By combining AMREF's expertise in healthcare with TSI's sports programs, we can now offer a more holistic approach to child development.

The team preparing for the game
TSI and AMREF teams in the athletics competition

What This Means for Our Children

Through this partnership, children in our programs will now have access to:

  • Regular health check-ups and medical screenings
  • Health education on nutrition, hygiene, and disease prevention
  • Mental health support and counseling services
  • Emergency medical care referrals when needed
  • Wellness workshops for families and communities

"This partnership is a game-changer. We can now ensure that children not only develop their athletic abilities but also have access to quality healthcare that will support their overall well-being."

— Willis Otieno, Team Leader, TSI

AMREF's Commitment

AMREF has been a leader in healthcare across Africa for decades. Their commitment to community health and their understanding of the challenges faced by children in informal settlements make them the ideal partner for TSI.

After sponsership, the team with their new award

Looking Ahead

The partnership will officially launch in July 2024, with pilot programs starting in three informal settlements. We will provide regular updates on the progress and impact of this collaboration.

We are incredibly excited about the possibilities this partnership brings. Together with AMREF, we are building a brighter, healthier future for the children we serve.
